# Stability: a bounded iPad soak - eight minutes of gameplay with input, a lifecycle round trip, no fault - 2026-09-23

Purpose: the acceptance bar asks for fresh gameplay with input, stability and lifecycle, not counters or a
title frame.  This is that run on the current build, bounded to sixteen ticks, with the lane's own signals
sampled every tick.

## Method

Boot the iPad lane, launch, drive the accepted route to Training (`replay-ios-normal-route.py`, exit 0), then
sixteen 30-second ticks of input phases through the shell's own hold triggers - stick holds of ~24 s in each
direction, D-pad taps, and Cross taps - with a lifecycle round trip in the middle (launch Settings to background
the app, relaunch it to bring it back), sampling per tick: `pad0 sample` lines (input reached the game),
`scene-level`, `ottsel-fault.log` size, and the tick's own module census, plus a screenshot every second tick.

## Boundary

Proven: on the current build the lane holds a bounded gameplay session with sustained input (the target moved
~4.8 M units), survives a background/foreground round trip and keeps taking input afterwards, and reports no
fault in eight minutes.  Not claimed: audibility (device gate), physical devices, or any device-side cadence -
the ~8 fps here is this Simulator's, and the frame-rate question belongs to the L3 measurement.
