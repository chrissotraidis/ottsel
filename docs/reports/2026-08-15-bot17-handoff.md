2. Then the sprite VU1 (2D sprites/fonts) and the merc/tie VU1s.
3. Then the direct rasterizer (already written) handles the output GIF
   packets; the framebuffer copies (tbp 0x2800/0x3000, 512x256) then have
   real content.

## Gotchas (this loop)

- REGLIST XYZF2 register = 64 bits; the observed values pack x in the low 16
  (0x7000..0x9000 = fullscreen width) with a constant 0x7910 field; the
  DirectRenderer2 math maps 0x8000->center, /0x1000 per NDC unit.
- VIF UNPACK_V4_32 = 0x6c in the tag's high byte; tfrag batches repeat a
  10-qword constant block, then big vertex batches (floats), 16-bit frag
  values, and NaN (0x7fc00000) terminators.
- Link-phase faults remain nondeterministic (~1-in-3); relaunch until the
  fault log is 0 bytes; the app holds at fault for lldb.
